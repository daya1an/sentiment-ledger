# SENTIENT-LEDGER: CORPORATE FINANCIAL & INVOICE APPROVAL POLICIES
# Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. INFRASTRUCTURE & CLOUD SERVICES
- Cloud infrastructure invoices (e.g., Azure Cloud, AWS, Google Cloud) under 5000.00 are automatically approved to ensure uninterrupted service.
- Infrastructure invoices exceeding 5000.00 require manual review by the IT Director.

2. SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTIONS
- Routine software subscriptions (SaaS) under 50.00 are auto-approved.
- Any software subscription 50.00 or higher must be flagged for manual review to prevent unauthorized shadow IT.

3. TRAVEL & ENTERTAINMENT
- All travel-related expenses (flights, hotels, cabs) require manual review regardless of the amount. Auto-approval is strictly prohibited for the TRAVEL category.
- Flight bookings must be economy class. Any invoice indicating business or first-class travel must be immediately rejected.

4. OFFICE SUPPLIES
- General office supplies under 200.00 are auto-approved.
- Office equipment (desks, chairs, monitors) exceeding 200.00 requires HR approval.

5. RESTRICTED & PROHIBITED VENDORS
- Any invoice from a vendor categorized as "Cryptocurrency", "Gambling", or "Unregistered" must be instantly rejected.
- Invoices from the vendor "Unknown LLC" must be rejected due to a pending legal dispute.

6. GENERAL RULES
- If an invoice category does not explicitly match any of the above policies, it defaults to requiring a manual review.
- No invoice with a negative or zero amount can be processed; these must be rejected.
